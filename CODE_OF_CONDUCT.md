# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should keep technical discussion professional, specific, and evidence-based.

Expected behavior includes:

- addressing ideas and technical decisions rather than people;
- providing reproducible evidence for defects and regressions;
- accepting review and correction without hostility;
- distinguishing confirmed behavior from assumptions;
- protecting credentials, personal data, and security-sensitive information;
- respecting project scope and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, or knowingly unsafe technical guidance is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.

Project-specific conduct rules may supplement this document.
